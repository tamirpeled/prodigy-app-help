Android test invitations

Each product guide has an email link to request a US or Canadian test invitation. We use that request only for the app named in your message. We will reply with availability and participation details. You can withdraw by emailing the same address.

Restore Pro

Sign in to the same store account used for purchase, open the original app’s subscription screen and select Restore purchases. An internet connection is required to refresh the store entitlement. A store purchase restores Pro access. Your saved work lives on the device where you created it.

Manage a monthly subscription

On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, choose Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose the relevant app to view the renewal date or cancel. Uninstalling an app leaves renewal active until you cancel in the store.

Keeping a copy of your work

Use the app’s share action to save an estimate, handoff, batch sheet, market report or lesson statement to a destination you trust. The exported report is a readable copy. Store it before you clear app data or change devices.
