Product help

How does the temporary Pro workspace work?

Open Subscription & support and choose Try a temporary Pro workspace. You can try the paid workflow with sample records. Exiting clears your preview work and returns you to your regular records.

Why do pack units and recipe units have to match?

The calculation divides the recipe quantity by the purchase-pack quantity. Use grams for both quantities or choose another matching unit before calculating.

Where do I find my records?

Open OvenWeft on the device where you saved the work. Sharing sends a copy of the selected output to the destination you choose. Keep a copy of important reports before clearing the app’s data.
