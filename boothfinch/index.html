Getting started

  1. Open a market with a date, currency and booth fee. Add the stock and unit costs you packed.
  2. Record each sale. The tally updates your sold count and the amount left to cover the booth fee.
  3. Close the market and share the day’s report.

Product help

How does the temporary Pro workspace work?

Open Subscription & support and choose Try a temporary Pro workspace. You can try the paid workflow with sample records. Exiting clears your preview work and returns you to your regular records.

Does the sales tally charge my customer?

Use your existing cash or card method to collect the sale. Record it in BoothFinch to update your inventory, event totals and fee coverage.

Where do I find my records?

Open BoothFinch on the device where you saved the work. Sharing sends a copy of the selected output to the destination you choose. Keep a copy of important reports before clearing the app’s data.
