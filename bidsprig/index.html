Product help

How does the temporary Pro workspace work?

Open Subscription & support and choose Try a temporary Pro workspace. You can try the paid workflow with sample records. Exiting clears your preview work and returns you to your regular records.

How do I change the currency?

Choose USD or CAD when editing an estimate. Enter amounts in that currency. The currency selector changes how values are labelled.

Where do I find my records?

Open BidSprig on the device where you saved the work. Sharing sends a copy of the selected output to the destination you choose. Keep a copy of important reports before clearing the app’s data.
