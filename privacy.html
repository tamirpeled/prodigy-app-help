Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Who this covers

Prodigy-AI offers BidSprig, StayBaton, OvenWeft, BoothFinch and PackTern for adults managing their own work. This policy covers these five apps and their product and support pages. Send privacy questions or deletion requests to tamir.peleds@gmail.com.

Records on your device

BidSprig stores saved estimates, names, rates and notes. StayBaton stores property names, tasks, dates and handoff notes. OvenWeft stores recipes, ingredients, yields and costs. BoothFinch stores market inventory, costs and sales tallies. PackTern stores learner names or nicknames, subjects, attendance and lesson credits. Enter only the details you need for the work.

The apps keep these records in app storage on your device. Prodigy-AI has no account or hosted copy of your work records. Android app backup is disabled for these apps. On Apple devices, system backup settings may apply. Review your device’s backup and screen-lock settings if you store information about other people.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, the app uses the store’s purchase service. The store processes your account and payment details and returns transaction information and subscription status so the app can unlock, verify or restore Pro. The publisher receives store financial and subscription reports. Your card details stay with your payment provider. Stores retain their own billing records under their own terms. On Android, Google’s billing library can send Google technical diagnostics about billing requests, including response codes and error messages. The apps do not attach your saved work records to these requests.

Sharing and support

When you choose Share, the app passes the selected report, text or document to your device’s sharing service. The recipient or destination app receives what you send and controls its own copies. Review a report before sharing it.

If you email support, the publisher and its email provider receive your email address and the message and attachments you provide. We use them to answer and resolve your request. Please send a description or a redacted screenshot where possible. Avoid including client, student or payment details.

If you request an Android test invitation, we use the email and app name you provide to reply about that test and, if you choose to participate, manage access through Google Play. Google receives the Play account email when we add it to an email-based tester list. Test requests are separate for each app and do not enroll you in a general mailing list. You can email support to withdraw.

Website and diagnostics

The free website calculators run in your browser. They do not submit the amounts you enter to Prodigy-AI. The website does not keep a calculation history. The product pages contain no advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The hosting provider processes requests, including IP addresses and technical information, to deliver the pages and operate the service. The apps have no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. Apple or Google may make aggregate store and diagnostic reports available to the publisher according to your device and store settings.

Retention and deletion

Saved work remains on your device until you remove the records, clear the app’s storage or delete the app. Before removing data, share a copy of any report you need. On iOS, choose Delete App to remove its local data. Device backup and recipient copies have separate deletion controls. Removing the app leaves management of any subscription with your store account.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve requests and meet applicable recordkeeping duties. Email tamir.peleds@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of support information the publisher holds. The publisher cannot retrieve local records from your device. If this policy changes, this page will show the revised effective date.
