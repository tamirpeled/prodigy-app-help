Getting started

  1. Add the learner’s name or nickname and the prepaid credits currently available.
  2. Log attendance and choose whether an absence uses a credit. Add another pack when the family renews.
  3. Open the family statement and share the learner’s dated balance.

Product help

How does the temporary Pro workspace work?

Open Subscription & support and choose Try a temporary Pro workspace. You can try the paid workflow with sample records. Exiting clears your preview work and returns you to your regular records.

Can I correct an attendance mistake?

Open the learner, find the history entry and choose Correct entry. Review and reverse it. Add the correct entry if needed. The family statement uses the corrected balance.

Where do I find my records?

Open PackTern on the device where you saved the work. Sharing sends a copy of the selected output to the destination you choose. Keep a copy of important reports before clearing the app’s data.
