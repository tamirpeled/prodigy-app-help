Getting started

  1. Add a property and list the tasks that make it ready.
  2. Schedule a turnover with the property, date and deadline. Check tasks off as work is completed.
  3. Add any handoff notes and share the status with your chosen recipient.

Product help

How does the temporary Pro workspace work?

Open Subscription & support and choose Try a temporary Pro workspace. You can try the paid workflow with sample records. Exiting clears your preview work and returns you to your regular records.

Does a handoff update on the recipient’s phone?

The share action sends a snapshot of the current checklist and notes. Share a fresh handoff after additional work is finished.

Where do I find my records?

Open StayBaton on the device where you saved the work. Sharing sends a copy of the selected output to the destination you choose. Keep a copy of important reports before clearing the app’s data.
